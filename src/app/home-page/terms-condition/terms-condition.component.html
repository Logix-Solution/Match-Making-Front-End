Accepting these terms

By registering an account, browsing profiles, or using any part of Nadia's Matchmaking Services, you agree to these Terms & Conditions along with our Privacy Policy. If you don't agree with any part of them, please don't create an account or continue using the platform.

02

Who can join

This platform is built for adults genuinely seeking marriage or a serious, committed relationship. To register, you must:

  • Meet the platform's minimum age requirement in your country of residence.
  • Be legally free to marry — not currently in an undissolved marriage, unless the plan or profile type you're using explicitly permits otherwise.
  • Provide accurate information about yourself during registration and profile setup.
  • Use the platform for yourself, or on behalf of a family member with their knowledge and consent.
03

Your account & responsibilities

Your account is personal to you. That comes with a few responsibilities:

  • Keep your credentials private — you're responsible for any activity that happens under your login.
  • Keep your profile accurate — update your details if your circumstances change (marital status, location, or anything material to how you're matched).
  • One profile per person — duplicate or fake accounts will be removed.
  • Report concerns promptly — if you suspect a profile is fraudulent or another member has misrepresented themselves, tell us right away.
04

Plans, fees & payments

Some features — like unlocking contact details or sending unlimited interest — require a paid membership plan.

  • Fees are shown clearly before you confirm any purchase, in the currency applicable to your account.
  • Plans renew or expire according to the duration you selected at checkout; we don't auto-upgrade you to a costlier tier without your action.
  • Refunds are considered case-by-case where a technical error prevented you from using a plan's core features — reach out to support to request one.
  • Payment processing is handled by our licensed payment partners; we never store your raw card details on our servers.
Prices and included features may change over time. If you're on an active paid plan, changes won't retroactively affect the term you've already paid for.
05

Code of conduct

Matchmaking only works if people can trust what they see. We expect every member to:

  • Communicate respectfully, even when a conversation doesn't lead anywhere.
  • Represent themselves honestly — real photos, real details, real intentions.
  • Respect a "no" — repeated unwanted contact after someone has declined interest is not tolerated.
  • Keep conversations on-platform until both sides are comfortable moving forward, for everyone's safety.

Prohibited activities

The following will result in content removal, suspension, or a permanent ban, depending on severity:

  • Creating a fake, duplicate, or impersonating profile.
  • Uploading photos that aren't genuinely and recently of you.
  • Harassment, hate speech, threats, or any form of abusive behaviour toward another member.
  • Using the platform for commercial solicitation, advertising, matchmaking services outside our own, or any purpose other than finding a personal match.
  • Requesting or sending money from another member under any circumstance.
  • Attempting to scrape, copy, or misuse other members' profile data.
