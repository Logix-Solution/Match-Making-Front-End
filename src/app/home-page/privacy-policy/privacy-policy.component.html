01

Information we collect

To bring two people together well, we need to actually know something about each of them. Here's what we ask for, and why each piece matters:

  • Account details — your name, contact number, email, and the credentials that keep your account yours alone.
  • Profile information — age, height, marital status, occupation, education, and the preferences you set for a future partner. This is the raw material of every match we suggest.
  • Photos & documents — profile photos you choose to upload, and, where relevant, verification documents used to confirm you are who you say you are.
  • Activity signals — the profiles you view, the interests you send, and the messages you exchange, which help our matching quality improve over time.
  • Payment details — handled by our payment partners when you choose a paid plan; we store the plan and transaction record, never your raw card details.
  • Device & usage data — general technical information like browser type and approximate location, mainly used to keep the platform secure and working properly.
Sensitive fields — like religion, sect, or family background where you choose to share them — are only ever used to power matching and are never repurposed for advertising.
02

How we use your information

Every use of your data traces back to one of these purposes:

  • Suggesting matches that reflect your stated preferences and profile.
  • Letting you send and receive interest, and message people you've mutually connected with.
  • Verifying accounts and keeping fake or duplicate profiles off the platform.
  • Processing plan upgrades and keeping your billing history accurate.
  • Improving how matches are ranked and presented, using aggregated, de-identified patterns — never your identity attached to marketing lists.
  • Responding when you reach out to our support team.
Never sold to advertisers Never shared for marketing Used only to serve you
03

Profile visibility & matching

Matchmaking only works with a degree of visibility — but you stay in control of how much of yourself is shown, and to whom.

  • Default visibility is limited to the platform's suggested matches; your profile is not indexed by search engines or visible to the public internet.
  • Contact details — phone number and address — stay hidden from other members until you choose to reveal them, typically once interest has been exchanged and accepted.
  • Photos can be set to visible-to-all-matches or blurred-until-interest-accepted, depending on your comfort level.
  • Hiding your profile temporarily is always available from your account settings, without needing to delete anything.
04

When we share information

We share information only in the narrow set of situations below, and never as a way to monetize your data:

  • With other members — only the profile details you've chosen to make visible, and only within the platform's matching flow.
  • With service providers — payment processors, cloud hosting, and messaging tools that operate the platform on our behalf, bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • For legal reasons — if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our members.
  • With your explicit consent — for any purpose outside the above, we'll always ask first.
05

Keeping your data secure

We apply layered safeguards — encrypted connections, access controls limiting who on our team can view profile data, and regular review of how information flows through the platform. No system is unbreakable, so we also monitor actively for unusual activity and will notify affected members promptly if something ever goes wrong.

06

How long we keep your information

We keep your profile and activity data for as long as your account is active, so matching continues to work well. If you delete your account, we remove your profile from active matching immediately and fully erase personal data within a reasonable retention window, except where a longer period is required for legal, security, or billing-record purposes.

08

Cookies & similar technology

We use a small set of cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand which features of the platform are actually useful. We don't use cookies to build advertising profiles or track you across unrelated websites.

09

A note on age

Nadia's Matchmaking Services is built for adults seeking marriage or a serious relationship, and registration is restricted to users who meet our platform's minimum age requirement. If we learn that an account belongs to someone below that age, we will close it and remove the associated data.
