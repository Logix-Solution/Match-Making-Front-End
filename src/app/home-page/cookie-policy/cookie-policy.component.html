The short version: we use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand which parts of the platform people actually use. We don't use cookies to build advertising profiles or sell your browsing activity to anyone.
01

What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that your browser stores when you visit a website. It lets the site remember something about your visit the next time you load a page — like whether you're signed in, or what settings you last chose. Cookies can't read files on your device or run programs; they simply hold small pieces of information tied to your browser.

02

Types we use

  • Essential cookies — required for the platform to function at all: staying signed in, keeping your session secure, and remembering the page you were on.
  • Preference cookies — remember choices like your currency, language, or display settings so you don't have to reset them every visit.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand, in aggregate, which features are used and where people get stuck, so we can improve the platform.
No advertising cookies No cross-site tracking No data resale
03

Cookie details

A closer look at what's actually stored and for how long:

Cookie Purpose Duration
session_token Keeps you signed in securely between page loads Session
auth_refresh Renews your session without requiring you to log in again 30 days
pref_currency Remembers the currency used for plan pricing 1 year
pref_lang Remembers your selected display language 1 year
analytics_id Anonymized identifier used to understand feature usage in aggregate 2 years
04

Third-party cookies

A small number of cookies are set by trusted service providers we use to run the platform — for example, our payment processor during checkout, or our analytics provider. These partners are bound by confidentiality obligations and are not permitted to use the data for their own advertising purposes.

05

Your choices

You're in control of cookies at the browser level:

  • Most browsers let you view, delete, or block cookies from their settings menu.
  • Blocking essential cookies will likely prevent you from staying signed in or using core features of the platform.
  • Blocking preference or analytics cookies won't affect your ability to use the platform — you'll just need to reset preferences more often, and we'll have less insight into how to improve things.
Clearing your cookies will sign you out of the platform and reset any saved preferences, but won't affect your profile, matches, or messages — those live on our servers, not your browser.
06

Changes to this policy

If the cookies we use change in a meaningful way — for example, if we add a new analytics or payment partner — we'll update this page and, where appropriate, let you know directly through the app.
